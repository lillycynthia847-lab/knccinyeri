About Us

The Voice of Business
in Nyeri County

The Kenya National Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KNCCI) Nyeri Chapter is the leading business membership organisation representing the interests of businesses operating in Nyeri County and its environs.

We serve as the bridge between the private sector and government — advocating for policies that create an enabling environment for businesses to thrive and contribute to Nyeri's economic development.

  • Government-recognised business chamber
  • Affiliated to the national KNCCI body
  • Active membership across all business sectors
  • Strong partnerships with county and national government
